PRIVACY / LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Your photos. On your devices.

This policy explains data handling in Siftroom on Mac, iPhone and iPad, and on siftroom.com. We minimize collection and use only what is needed for a specific function.

Effective: November 20, 2026Version: 1.6

01 / ON YOUR DEVICE

Your photos stay on your devices

Siftroom does not require a Siftroom account, include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, or upload your photos, thumbnails, EXIF, ratings, adjustments or usage history to our servers.

On-device processing

Image decoding, culling analysis, face detection, AI denoise, portrait retouching, reference color matching and Astro processing run on the Mac, iPhone or iPad you use. Models are provided with the app; photos are not uploaded to our servers for inference.

Ratings, adjustments, project indexes, import records, filters and masks are stored locally. Filter packs contain declarative settings and assets; the app does not execute scripts, native plug-ins or third-party models from them.

Photo and file permissions

On iPhone and iPad, Siftroom reads the photos you select in the system picker and creates local working copies. Saving to Photos requests permission to add photos. You authorize folders and external storage through the system file picker. File access on Mac is also based on your selections and permissions.

Import and export use the locations you choose, and originals remain unchanged. Your chosen photo library or cloud drive may be synchronized by its system service. You decide whether to share exported photos, filter packs or client selection PDFs.

When editing through the Apple Photos extension, the system provides the selected photo and its adjustment data. The extension processes it on the device and returns the result and adjustment data to Apple Photos for saving.

Optional maps and location

When you use maps, place search or reverse geocoding, Apple’s map and geocoding services may receive your search text or coordinates. Current location is provided by the device’s location service with your permission. These features do not use Siftroom servers; you can browse, cull, denoise and export without location permission.

Location information can be stored in local settings and written to the GPS metadata of exported copies when you choose. You can turn off location writing or manage location access in system settings.

Pro purchases and suggestions

Pro purchases and restoration are handled by Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Siftroom uses Apple-verified transactions to determine access and does not receive card details or payment passwords. Usage dates, display times and preferences used for occasional Pro suggestions are stored only locally; suggestions can be turned off in Settings.

02 / THE WEBSITE

No tracking analytics on the website

siftroom.com uses no advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, profiling, third-party analytics scripts or social pixels. Static resources are served from Siftroom's own server.

Standard security and diagnostic access logs may include time, request path, response status, browser type and source IP. Logs are not used for advertising or profiling.

03 / SUPPORT REQUESTS

Information only when you ask for help

When you submit the support form, we process the name, contact email, subject and question you provide, and generate a ticket number. Do not send original photos, identity documents, passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive data.

Our server uses its configured email service to send requests to the Siftroom support inbox. The provider processes the information as needed to deliver email. Messages are used only for support notifications and replies, not marketing. Information is not sold or rented.

04 / RETENTION

Keep it only as long as needed

  • Support requests: up to 90 days, unless longer retention is needed to continue handling the same issue or meet legal obligations.
  • Web access logs: scheduled for rotation and deletion within 30 days.
  • Local app data is under your control. Mac work data is stored in Application Support or XMP files; mobile working copies and settings are stored in the app’s local storage. Deleting the app does not delete separately saved exports or photos in your library.

05 / YOUR CHOICES

Access, correction and deletion

Request access, correction or deletion through Support. We may ask you to confirm the ticket number from the original contact address to avoid changing someone else's records.

You can stop using the app or website at any time. Photo processing does not depend on a cloud account.

06 / SECURITY

Encrypted transport and limited access

The website uses HTTPS. The support endpoint limits request size and rate; records live in a restricted server directory. No system guarantees absolute security. If an incident could affect your security, we will act as required by applicable law.

07 / CHANGES

Policy changes

We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. The effective date appears on this page, with reasonable notice of significant changes.

08 / CONTACT

Contact us

For privacy questions or data requests, use the Siftroom support page and mention Privacy Request in the subject.
