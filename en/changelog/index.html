iPhone / iPad1.2.0

Edit in Photos and save to your library.

  • In Photos, tap Edit and More to open Siftroom. Adjust light and color or try five included filters for free.
  • View and export HDR for free. Automatic export saves supported HDR photos as HEIF and standard photos as JPEG.
  • Series Consistency is included in Pro. Pick a reference, review each preview, and apply to selected photos. Undo the batch in one step.
  • Preview all eight Signature Filters for free and unlock the pack separately to apply them. Filter Workshop is now free.
  • Fixed an issue that could flip some cached AI denoise results vertically.
Mac2.4.0

Start editing in Photos.

  • Choose Edit → Extensions → Siftroom in Photos to edit a still photo for free and save it back to the library.
  • View and export HDR for free. Automatic export saves supported HDR photos as HEIF and standard photos as JPEG.
  • Series Consistency is included in Pro. Pick a reference, review each preview, and apply to selected photos. Undo the batch in one step.
  • Preview all eight Signature Filters for free and unlock the pack separately to apply them. Filter Workshop is now free.
  • Fixed an issue that could flip some cached AI denoise results vertically.
iPhone / iPad1.0.0

Siftroom comes to iPhone and iPad.

  • Open photos from Photos or Files. Zoom in on both images together in Compare, then touch and hold to swap them or copy edits from one to the other.
  • Edit color, crop, apply filters, retouch portraits and run on-device AI denoise in one workspace. Save finished photos to Photos or Files.
  • Save selected originals or create a client selection PDF. Pro includes Smart Culling, Smart Workshop, saving filters from reference photos, and star alignment.
Mac2.2.2

See adjustments as you drag.

  • Photos update as you drag sliders for color, filters and portrait retouching.
  • After Smart Culling, copy selected originals to a separate folder, optionally rename the copies, and keep paired RAW and JPEG files together.
  • Reuse existing AI denoise results when switching photos or exporting to reduce repeated processing.
  • Align the largest, centered and all-faces mask template options, and improve duplicate-name handling when importing client selection PDFs.
2026.11.112.2.1

AI DENOISE / PREVIEW

More reliable AI denoise previews.

  • Fixed a bug that could repeatedly denoise the same photo in the preview when processing large images or when memory was tight.
2026.11.102.2.0

PORTRAITS / NIGHT SKY

From portrait detail to the night sky.

  • New on-device Portrait Retouch. Natural smoothing, even skin tone, and gentle slimming are free. Select faces in group photos and zoom in for a before-and-after comparison.
  • Pro adds separate cheek and jawline controls and batch application of smoothing and even skin tone.
  • Star Trails and Align Stars share the Astro workspace. Free Star Trails adds Comet Tails with adjustable fade strength and direction.
  • Star alignment rechecks some borderline matches at full resolution. Processing records list the frames used and reasons for exclusion.
2.1.1

ONE WORKSPACE / NEW WAYS TO CREATE

Open a folder. Stay with the photo.

  • A unified workspace keeps browsing, adjustments, reference colors and night-sky processing around the current collection, with less reselecting and window switching.
  • Controls and the filmstrip appear when needed. Use ⌘/ to keep controls visible and ⌘0 to return to photos.
  • New reference-based filters offer a free preview, adjustable strength, optional tone matching and skin protection. Pro adds saved filter packs and batch application.
  • New basic Star Trails offers free previews and 16-bit TIFF export. Pro adds foreground protection with brush and eraser refinement.
  • Improved mask-brush undo, TIFF replacement, access to composite results and background-task progress.
  • The new Pro features are included for existing owners, with no additional purchase.
2026.11.042.0.0

SIFTROOM 2.0 / LIFETIME PRO

Culling, creativity and delivery.
Connected on your Mac.

  • New lifetime Pro: Intelligent Culling, Smart Workshop, advanced filter creation and Star Stacking. No subscription.
  • Group candidates, inspect sharpness and face hints, compare details and save culling progress. You make the final choice.
  • Reference colors and Apple on-device masks become editable layers. Filter Workshop adds HSL, three-way grading, custom test photos and multi-photo previews.
  • Star Stacking adds alignment, multiple linear-light stacking methods, foreground masks and brush refinement, with 16-bit TIFF output.
  • New photo versions, delivery profiles and Task Center; improved workshop undo, redo and draft recovery.
  • Language changes apply immediately, with improved localized menus and automatic project/template names.
  • Browsing, manual culling, import, projects, local AI denoising and export remain free.

2026.10.271.1.0

IMPORT / PROJECTS / LOCAL DENOISE

From memory card
to photos worth keeping.

  • New Import Center: preview and import a card or folder, with per-file SHA-256 read-back verification.
  • Optional second-disk backup with independent verification, pause and resume, and exportable reports. Sources remain intact.
  • Grouped RAW/JPEG/XMP renaming, identical-content deduplication and metadata templates for creator, copyright and keywords.
  • New Projects: remember review progress, edits and your last photo, reuse local analysis and relocate moved folders.
  • New on-device AI Denoise with strength, luminance and color controls, native-pixel comparison and batch export of picked photos.
  • Refined import layout, date and option controls, denoise feedback and detail comparison.
