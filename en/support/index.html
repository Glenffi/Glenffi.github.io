01 / START

The instructions below are for Mac. For iPhone and iPad, see “Mac and mobile”.

Getting started

Find the controls

Reveal controls near the canvas edges, or press ⌘/ to keep them visible. Culling, color and compositing stay in the workspace; press ⌘0 to return to photos.

Open your photos

Drag photos or a folder into Siftroom, or press ⌘O. Enable the optional Finder Service in System Settings.

Start culling

Browse with Left/Right Arrow. P picks, X rejects; press the same key again to clear. U always clears the flag.

Compare detail

Select two photos and press C. Pinch to zoom and pan with two fingers; both photos stay in sync.

Batch delivery

Adjust a selection or apply a preset together, then export with a base name and padded sequence.

Add a watermark

Press ⌘/ and choose Actions → Watermark & Location to set a watermark and capture location while browsing. Preview again before export, or use ⌘⇧W for watermarked export.

Client proofing

Choose Export Client Selection in Export. Clients check the PDF, save and return it; import the returned PDF to flag the matching originals.

Use local filters

Open Window → Filter Library to import a .siftfilterpack file, or create RGB and mask-based filters in the workshop.

Lightweight development

Exposure, tone, white balance, clarity, dehaze, denoising and sharpening are non-destructive.

MAC / IPHONE / IPAD

One workflow, shaped for each screen.

Mac works with folders, a keyboard and a larger screen. iPhone and iPad start with Photos, with Files available for folders and external storage.

How do I open tools on iPhone and iPad?

Tap the photo to reveal the controls, then open Edit and Tools. iPhone uses a bottom panel; iPad uses a side inspector in landscape. Close the panel to return to your photograph.

Do photos and edits sync automatically across devices?

Siftroom does not provide its own cloud library or automatic project sync. Use your chosen file storage, exported photos, filter packs and XMP files to move work between devices. Check that edits and external assets are complete after importing.

Where do mobile exports go?

The photo view’s save button saves to Photos by default. The Delivery panel can also save to Files. Client selection PDFs use the system share or file-saving interface. Originals remain unchanged.

Where can I find the examples?

On Mac, choose Help → Examples. On iPhone and iPad, open Examples from the empty workspace or the Photos and Projects menu. Follow tap, pinch, swipe, and touch-and-hold examples for your device layout.

IMPORT / VERIFY / CONTINUE

Start from a card.
Or open a folder directly.

Choose a card or folder in File → Import Photos… (⌘I). Preview source thumbnails, select a work disk and project, and set naming rules and optional metadata. You can still press ⌘O to review a folder without creating a project.

How do verification and the second copy work?

Each file is copied to a staging location, then read back and verified with SHA-256 before being published as a completed primary. A second copy is made from that verified primary and checked independently. Choose another volume for the backup, preferably on a separate physical disk so one disk failure cannot affect both copies. Verification does not replace a long-term backup strategy.

What if an import is interrupted? When can I eject the card?

Continue unfinished jobs from import history. Siftroom rechecks completed files and restarts an unfinished file; reauthorize source or backup folders if needed. Wait until all required copies have completed and passed verification before ejecting your card. Siftroom does not delete source photos or format cards.

Do naming, deduplication or metadata change my source files?

Sources stay unchanged. RAW, JPEG and XMP files are renamed as a group at the destination. Optional content deduplication reuses identical imported groups without overwriting review or edit state. Templates fill empty fields by default. Original XMP is archived separately and working XMP merges creator, copyright and keywords. The second-disk backup retains source bytes.

Will I lose progress if I move a photo folder?

Open File → Projects… (⌘⇧P), select the project and choose Relocate… to point to its new photo folder. Flags, edits, the last viewed photo and local analysis stay with the project. The list is a lightweight index; removing a project entry does not delete photos.

ON-DEVICE AI / NATIVE PIXELS

Denoise first. Then compare.

Expand AI Denoise in the inspector, increase strength and wait for processing. Choose Compare… to inspect the selected region at native pixels. Click the overview or use the arrows to move. Comparison is unavailable when denoising is off or still processing.

Are photos uploaded? Do I need a model download?

The AI denoise model is included. Denoising, image decoding, culling analysis, and export run on your device. Speed and results depend on the image size, noise, and hardware.

How do luminance, color and batch controls work?

Expand Luminance and Color to control how much of each type of noise is processed. Reduce luminance processing to retain more grain. Batch… can copy denoise settings to picked photos in the current folder, or process and export that set. Check the scope before exporting.

Why do some regions use protective processing?

The model may create unusual texture in very dark, smooth regions. Siftroom checks output and uses conservative denoising for clearly abnormal regions, with a notice in Compare. Inspect at native pixels before applying. This is not a specialized astrophotography denoiser or RAW mosaic reconstruction tool.

LOCAL STUDIO / PRO

Pro & Advanced Workflows

What is free, and what needs Pro?

The Apple Photos extension, HDR viewing and export, Filter Workshop, browsing and manual culling, import, projects, on-device AI denoise, basic retouching, and star trails are free. A one-time Pro purchase unlocks Client Proof Links, Series Consistency, Smart Culling, applying and saving Sample Filters, Smart Workshop, advanced face shaping, batch skin retouching, star alignment, and foreground protection. Preview all eight Signature Filters for free and unlock their pack separately to apply them.

How do I buy or restore Pro?

Open Siftroom → Siftroom Pro…. The App Store handles the purchase; no Siftroom account is needed. After reinstalling or moving to another Mac, sign into the App Store with the Apple Account used to buy Pro, then choose Restore Purchases. The purchase page shows your current storefront price.

Does Intelligent Culling delete photos for me?

No. Open Studio → Intelligent Culling… (⌘⌥A), analyze the photos and compare group-relative sharpness, exposure and face details. Split or merge groups and keep more than one frame. Confirmation marks only your checked photos. Eye hints are not definitive blink detection.

How do the workshops work?

In Studio → Smart Workshop, Pro matches reference colors or creates subject, person and background masks as editable layers. The free Filter Workshop supports your own RAW or JPEG test photos, previews on multiple photos, HSL and three-way color grading.

What photos should I use for Star Stacking?

Use at least two frames from the same sequence that pass assessment and alignment. For fixed-tripod shots, enable foreground protection, inspect the red area and refine it with the brush or an imported mask. Create a new 16-bit TIFF while preserving originals. Failed frames are excluded with a reason. This is not deep-sky calibration, star trails, HDR or focus stacking.

Where are versions, delivery profiles and tasks?

Use the Studio menu for Photo Versions, Delivery Profiles or Background Tasks (⌘⌥J). Versions retain different edits and crops; profiles reuse export settings. Task Center shows progress and history. Imports can resume; interrupted star stacks need to restart.

Do language changes require a restart?

No, starting in 2.0. Choose English, Simplified Chinese or Follow System under Settings → General → App Language. Open views update immediately. Your own saved project, template and filter names are not translated.

Can I turn off Pro suggestions?

Yes. Close the suggestion card, or choose Never Suggest Pro Again from its … menu. This permanently disables suggestions on this Mac. Suggestions are not shown while Pro is unlocked.

REFERENCE / REUSABLE LOOKS

Create your own reference-based filter

Open your photo collection, then choose Actions → Sample Filters or Studio → Create Filter from Sample…. Choose a reference and preview it on the current photo or your own test image. The reference and settings stay in place as you switch photos in the filmstrip.

What is free, and what needs Pro?

Choose a reference, compare the preview and adjust settings for free. Apply Sample Filters to photos, apply them in a batch or save them as a filter pack with Pro. Saved filters are available in the library and can be exported as .siftfilterpack files.

Why does it not look exactly like the reference?

Reference filters borrow color statistics and optional tone relationships. They do not copy the scene, lighting, depth of field or people. Adjust strength, then choose tone matching and skin protection based on the preview.

Do I need to choose the reference again for each photo?

No. The current reference and settings stay in place while the filmstrip changes the preview photo. Add other photos to the same workspace when needed.

Preview, apply and restore

Open a photo first. On Mac, choose Window → Filter Library…. On iPhone and iPad, open Adjustments → Filters. Select the Signature Filters tab and tap or click a look to preview it.

Adjust the strength or compare before and after for free. A separate one-time purchase unlocks applying all eight looks. To restore the pack, use the Apple Account used for purchase and choose Restore Purchases in the preview.

PORTRAIT / ON DEVICE

How do I use Portrait Retouch?

Where can I adjust skin and face shape?

Open a photo, press ⌘/ to show Actions, and choose Portrait Retouch. Adjust Natural Smoothing, Even Skin Tone, or Gentle Slimming. Choose a face in a group photo, and double-click to zoom before switching between Before and After.

Why is Gentle Slimming unavailable?

Siftroom shows the reason when a face is turned too far, close to the edge, or missing enough detected landmarks. Smoothing and even tone remain available for that face.

Which portrait adjustments can Pro apply in batches?

Select photos in the filmstrip, then batch-apply smoothing and even tone. Faces are detected separately, and group photos and uncertain detections are skipped. Face shaping is applied one photo at a time.

STAR TRAILS / STAR ALIGNMENT

Which Astro mode should I choose?

I want the trails stars trace over time

Open a fixed-tripod sequence, then choose Star Trails in Actions → Astro. Adjust the fade strength and direction of Comet Tails, or use standard trails at 0%. Preview the result and export a 16-bit TIFF.

I want point-like stars with less noise

Choose the Pro mode Align Stars in Actions → Astro. Evaluate and align stars, then stack at least two photos that pass the checks. The processing record lists used frames, reasons for exclusion, and alignment diagnostics.

How does star-trail foreground protection work?

Enable Protect Foreground · Pro and choose a clean foreground frame. Check the protected area, add with the brush or remove with the eraser. Hold Option to erase temporarily; ⌘Z undoes the last stroke. Other photos continue contributing to the sky.

How do I view or edit the exported result?

After a star-trail export, the composite appears in the same workspace. Choose Open in Main Window to continue editing the photo. Processing records retain the inputs and output information.

03 / FAQ

FAQ

Why does a RAW file show only its embedded preview?

RAW support depends on the camera, compression mode and system decoder version. Siftroom tries the system RAW decoder, then LibRaw. If neither supports the file, it keeps the embedded preview and labels its source clearly.

Do flags and edits change my originals?

No. Ratings, labels and edits are stored locally, or in a matching XMP sidecar when enabled. Pixel changes are written to new files only when exported.

How do I position a watermark?

Open watermark settings while browsing, or use the live preview in Export. Drag the mark freely or use the nine quick anchors. Position, size and opacity are remembered.

How do I send photos to a client for selection?

Choose Export Client Selection in Export. Send the generated selection PDF to your client. They check photos, save and return the PDF. Choose File → Import Client Picks… in Siftroom to mark the corresponding originals.

Why is Open in Siftroom missing from Finder?

Put Siftroom in Applications and launch it once. Go to System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → Services → Files and Folders, and enable Open in Siftroom.

Why will the filmstrip not scroll?

It scrolls only when its content is wider than the available space. Place the pointer over it and swipe with two fingers horizontally or vertically. If all collapsed groups fit, there is no extra distance to scroll.

Can I restore photos deleted on Mac?

Delete moves photos to the macOS Trash. Press ⌘Z immediately to undo, or restore them from the Trash. Siftroom watches the folder and updates its contents.

How do I import, create or share filters?

Open Window → Filter Library to import a local .siftfilterpack file, or create a filter in the workshop. Exported packs are ordinary files you can share through forums, cloud drives or messaging apps. Siftroom has no upload marketplace.

Can filter packs execute third-party code?

No. Packs contain only supported declarative RGB, LUT and mask parameters. Size, type and integrity are checked before import. Packs contain no scripts, plug-in binaries or executable models.

Are adjustment values compatible with Adobe Camera Raw?

No. Siftroom uses its own processing engine: exposure in EV, region-based tone controls and adaptive midtone contrast. It is designed for quick review and batch consistency, without claiming to reproduce Adobe's proprietary camera profiles or Process Version.

Does Siftroom upload photos or collect usage data?

Browsing, culling analysis, and image processing run on your device. When you publish a client proof album, the service receives watermarked previews and your client’s selection. Maps and geocoding use Apple services. The support form processes the information you choose to send.

Read the client proofing privacy policy

Where do I set the capture location?

With a photo open, choose Actions → Watermark & Location → Capture Location. Search for a place, choose a map point, or select This Mac to request system location permission. Locations are written to exported copies; the Mac’s current location is not necessarily where the photo was taken.

Share a proof link and collect picks

Client Proof Links require Siftroom 2.4.1 or later on Mac, or 1.2.1 or later on iPhone and iPad.

Mac

Open a photo project and choose File → Client Proof Links….

iPhone / iPad

Open a project and choose Edit and Tools → Studio → Delivery → Client Proof Links….

  1. Publishing requires Siftroom Pro. Sign in with Apple the first time, choose the photos and album name, then upload the previews to generate a link. Siftroom uploads watermarked JPEGs and keeps the originals on your device.
  2. Send the link to your client. They can open it on their phone or computer, select photos, and submit their picks in a browser without installing an app or creating an account.
  3. When the selection arrives, open the project containing the originals, review the album results, and choose Mark as Keep. Siftroom prompts you to check any originals that are missing, duplicated, or changed.

Publish up to 10 albums per UTC calendar month, with up to 300 previews per album. Links are active for 30 days after publication and can be withdrawn early. Share each link with the people choosing the photos.

How proofing data is handled

Use Siftroom in Apple Photos

Mac

Double-click a still photo in Photos, click Edit and choose Siftroom from Extensions (…). When you finish editing, click Save Changes. If Siftroom is missing, enable it under Photos Editing extensions in System Settings.

iPhone / iPad

Open a still photo, then tap Edit and More (…). Open Extensions if listed, then choose Siftroom. Tap Done in the extension first, then tap Done in Photos.

Adjust light and color or try five included filters for free. If the original is still in iCloud, download it in Photos first.

Choose HDR or SDR output

Open a photo with HDR data in Siftroom. An HDR-capable display shows highlight brightness beyond the SDR range.

Mac

Press ⌘E to open export settings and choose an output format.

iPhone / iPad

Open Edit and Tools → Studio → Delivery. Choose a format and save to Photos or Files.

  • Preserve HDR Automatically. Exports supported HDR photos as HEIF (.heic) and standard photos as JPEG.
  • HEIF · HDR. Keeps supported HDR highlights in the exported HEIC.
  • JPEG · SDR. Maps HDR highlights to standard dynamic range for sharing across devices.

In the Photos extension, use Output to preserve HDR or choose SDR compatibility for supported photos. Saving HDR to the library requires Photos to accept HEIC.

Match photos from the same scene

In the main app, open at least two photos from the same scene. In Studio → Series Consistency · Pro, choose your edited reference and analyze the group. On iPhone or iPad, open Studio from Edit and Tools.

Review the before and after for each photo, adjust the group strength, then select the photos to update. On Mac, press ⌘Z to undo the batch. On iPhone or iPad, return to the viewer, touch and hold a photo, and choose Undo Last Action.
