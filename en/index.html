01 / A VERIFIED START

Bring photos home.
Keep peace of mind.

Preview a card or folder, then choose your work disk. Every copied file is read back and verified. Add a second copy on another volume when you need one; it is verified independently. Your source photos stay intact.

01 / VERIFYCopy. Then read it back.

Per-file SHA-256 verification, with an exportable report. Use a separate physical disk for your second copy.

02 / RESUMEInterrupted? Continue.

Pause or quit, then resume from import history. Start culling primary files that have already passed verification.

03 / ORGANIZEOne shoot. One set of rules.

Rename RAW, JPEG and XMP groups together. Save creator, copyright and keywords in metadata templates, and skip identical content.

02 / A PLACE FOR EACH SHOOT

Come back tomorrow.
Continue today's decisions.

Keep each shoot in its own project, with flags, edits, analysis and your last viewed photo. Relocate the project after moving its folder. Opening a folder directly is always an option.

05 / ON-DEVICE AI DENOISE

Less noise.
Your eye decides.

The model is built into the app and processes photos on this Mac. Enable denoising, then inspect native-pixel detail. Adjust strength, luminance and color noise until the texture feels right.

Once you are happy, copy the settings to picked photos or process and export the set.

Results vary with the source and noise. Compare at 100% to choose the right strength for each photo.

06 / LOCAL FILTER LAB

Your look.
In a single file.

Import .siftfilterpack to apply RGB and mask-based filters. Packs contain declarative parameters and assets, never executable third-party code. Build and export your own in the workshop, then share the file wherever you like.

  • Seven official examples, with real rendered previews
  • Linear, radial, luminance, color and brush masks
  • An individual keyboard shortcut for every filter
